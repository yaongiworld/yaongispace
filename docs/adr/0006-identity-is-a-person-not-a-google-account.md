# Identity is a Person; a Google account is only a credential

Data ownership is never keyed to a Google account id. Every row belongs to a **Person** —
our own entity — and a Google account is a credential *attached to* a Person, with more
than one able to attach.

The obvious shortcut would be to treat the Google account as the identity, since Google
is currently the only way to sign in. We deliberately did not.

## Consequences

If one of us loses a Google account, we have lost a credential and not half a shared
archive. This is what the *long-lived data* principle demands: the photos and letters
must outlive any particular login.

It also makes a break-glass sign-in (email magic link, passkey) a later addition rather
than a migration, and it is the same seam the future "Yaongichu!" voice ritual attaches
to — a greeting on a session that never really expired, gating nothing.
