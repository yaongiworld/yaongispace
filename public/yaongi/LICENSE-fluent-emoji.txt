The cat faces in this directory are PLACEHOLDERS.

They are Microsoft's Fluent Emoji 3D, used under the MIT licence below. They
are glyphs, not a mascot: the real yaongi is being commissioned as an original
drawing, and when it lands it ships one image per mood in `lib/mood.ts` and
these files go away. The mood *vocabulary* is the part meant to survive, not
these drawings.

Two rules for whoever replaces or adds to them:

  * No third-party character IP, ever. This app is meant to be public-able and
    showable in Yangcho's MBA portfolio, and an asset licensed for private use
    only would quietly take that away. MIT (Fluent) and Apache-2.0 (Noto) are
    the licences already cleared for this repo.
  * A licence file rides along with the asset, as it does for the fonts in
    `public/fonts` and the icons in `public/icons`.

Source: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
