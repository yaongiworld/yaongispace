Worth knowing before you settle: the mascot does not have to be one fixed image. Because it is a plain PNG swapped by state, the cat can have moods — heart-eyes when a letter is waiting, weary at 2am, tears of joy after a trip import. Fluent's cats are still Microsoft's drawings. If none of these feel like your yaongi, that is the strongest argument yet for commissioning an original — which also buys back the ability to make this app public one day.
